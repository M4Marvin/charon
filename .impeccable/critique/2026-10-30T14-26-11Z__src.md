## Design Specificity Verdict

**Split.** The conversation surface is unmistakably Charon: a full-bleed blurred portrait scrim, the character name in teal against narration set in warm-gold italic, glass bubbles, a right-edge tick rail, and a per-reply branch counter. No generic SaaS produces that. The management surfaces are category-generic — a portrait-card grid, two-column chat cards, a left-rail settings shell — and carry marketplace furniture (`by m4marvin` on every card, `@m4marvin` under the title, format badges in the tag row) that means nothing in a private single-user app. The app has a vocabulary for the story and none for the shelf.

**LLM assessment**: cohesion is high inside chat and drops sharply at the library/settings boundary. The one place the product's own premise should be loudest — branching — is the smallest, least labelled thing on screen.

**Deterministic scan** (v0.1.18): source scan exit 2 — 15 primary findings, 4 advisory. 14 `overused-font` hits in `src/fonts.css` are Inter and Fraunces `@font-face` declarations (two families counted once per face); runtime scans call Inter 87–94 % of page text. That is a taste flag, not a defect: Fraunces is the authored voice, Inter is the generic body. One `side-tab` hit (`src/styles.css:208`, `.rt-mes-text blockquote`) is a false positive — it is a quote bar on a semantic blockquote, and it does not reproduce at runtime. URL scans ran over Chromium's DevTools port on `/characters`, `/chat/chat-elias`, `/settings`, `/lorebooks`; the only genuine non-font finding is `skipped-heading` on Characters (`<h1>` → card `<h3>`, no `<h2>`; `src/routes/characters/index.tsx:467`). `content-hidden-at-rest` on Settings/Lorebooks is the dev-only TanStack Devtools overlay, not product copy. No contrast, tiny-text, spacing, heading-rhythm or overflow findings — but the detector is silent under 11 px, and `/characters` has 31 visible text nodes at 11 px (creator line, tags, `+4`, `/` hint). The measured palette keeps those labels at 8.5:1 (text-2 on base), so this is a size problem, not a contrast failure.

**Visual overlays**: injection succeeded headless on five pages (live-server :8400) and rendered the fixed banner (`div.impeccable-overlay.impeccable-banner`). There is no human-visible overlay: the runs were headless and timing-sensitive — one pass missed `/chat/chat-elias`'s font finding that the CDP scan caught.

## Overall Impression

Charon is two products in one shell. The writing room is authored, atmospheric, and quiet in a way that fits long-form roleplay; the library and settings are competent shadcn scaffolding wearing character-card jargon. The gap matters because the product's promise — every reply is a branch, your library comes with you — is carried by the weakest surfaces: import, the persona placeholder, and the branch counter. Fix three edges (mobile chat header, import failure, branch affordance) and the app will feel as deliberate at the shelf as it does in the scene.

## What's Working

1. **The reading surface has a point of view.** Portrait scrim + glass bubbles + teal name + gold italic narration is a literary grammar, not a chat-app skin, and it survives 768 px intact.
2. **The library is quiet in the right way.** 3:4 portraits on near-black, one accent, tags capped at three + `+N`, metadata on one line. Next to SillyTavern/Agnai density it reads like a shelf.
3. **Above-category component and a11y discipline.** Icon controls carry `aria-label`s, the active tab sets `aria-current="page"`, confirms focus Cancel, focus rings are visible 2 px brand, switches expand their hit area, and `message-glass` avoids per-row `backdrop-filter`.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Chat settings is off-screen on narrow phones.** At 320 px the header shows back, portrait, name and two of three buttons; the settings gear ends 52 px outside the viewport (12 px at 360 px). That gear is the only path to model, persona, lorebooks, prompts and display for the chat. **Why it matters:** on a phone the app cannot change model or persona at all. **Fix:** below `md`, collapse the three icon buttons into one 40 px menu (the spec's own "single View menu below xl"); check the name block's `min-w-0` chain. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`.

**[P1] Import failure is a dead end in jargon.** A 732×197 dropzone accepts any PNG, then answers with an `Error` panel reading "No PNG metadata." — no action, no example, no way forward. **Why it matters:** import is the front door; this is the moment a new user decides whether the app is for them. **Fix:** say "That PNG doesn't contain a character card. Export the card from its source, or try another file." with a "Choose a different file" action; reword the dropzone line to "Character card PNG (V2 or V3) · up to 50 MB". **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`.

**[P1] The defining feature is the smallest thing on screen.** Branching renders as `2/2` with chevrons at a bubble's bottom-right; at the newest variant the next chevron unmounts, leaving a lone `‹ 2/2`. The words "branch" and "alternate" never appear in the interface, and the message menu has no "Branch from here". **Why it matters:** the product's premise is invisible and unlearnable. **Fix:** "Alternate 2 of 2" with both chevrons always present (disabled at ends), a "Branch from here" menu item, and the existing aria-labels promoted to tooltips. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`.

**[P2] Every new user's voice is named "Default".** `src/server/seed.ts:406` seeds the persona as `"Default"` for every owner account, so user bubbles read "Default" with a "D" avatar — a placeholder in the middle of the fiction. **Why it matters:** it undercuts the intimacy the reading surface works hard to build. **Fix:** seed from the owner account (`name || username`), or render "You" when the persona has no name. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`.

**[P2] One destructive action, three confirmations.** The library shows `Delete "Lucien Vane"?` with a generic body; character detail shows `Delete character` with exact chat and message counts and straight quotes (`src/routes/characters/delete-stats.ts:8`); chat settings says "Permanently deletes …". **Why it matters:** the most consequential action is the least consistent, and the safest copy exists only on one of three entry points. **Fix:** one `ConfirmDialog` helper taking `{name, chatCount, messageCount}`, title `Delete "{name}"?`, used by all three. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** their own words are attributed to "Default"; the first failure says "No PNG metadata."; import guidance says "V2 or V3 card"; the branch control is `2/2`; format badges outrank story tags. Abandonment risk concentrates at import and the first reply.

**Mobile:** chat settings is clipped at ≤371 px with no alternative entry; provider cards truncate "Untest…" and the URL at 390 px; chat-list metadata truncates to "Dr. Margaret Finch · 1 …"; sign-in drops the wordmark.

**Keyboard / screen reader:** the authenticated shell has no skip link (only the landing page does, `src/routes/index.tsx:261`), so the first tabs are logo → Chats → Characters → Lorebooks → Search; the branch counter's accessible name is bare "2/2"; five separately tabbable icons per message mean 45 tab stops in a nine-message chat.

## Minor Observations

- Hover feedback exists but is imperceptible: a 1 px border to `brand/40` plus a 2 % portrait scale on cards, a `bg-base`→`bg-surface` shift on the dropzone.
- "3 characters" appears twice (subtitle + live-region count); a filtered empty state reads "0 characters" above "Nothing matches your search".
- Heading order on Characters: `<h1>` → `<h3>` card titles, no `<h2>` (detector `skipped-heading`, genuine).
- Eleven-pixel metadata (31 nodes on `/characters`) sits at the functional floor; legible, but it makes the shelf feel smaller than the reading room.
- The settings H1 is always "Settings" and repeats its subtitle 130 px later on Providers; the left rail says "Preferences", the page says "Settings", the section says "Display preferences".
- Document titles are generic ("Chat · Charon"), so browser history is a row of identical tabs.
- Branch chevrons are asymmetric at the newest variant; the marketing capture's richer composer placeholder ("Message Captain Elias Thorne…") was dropped in `composer.tsx:171`.
- The floating bottom-right button in dev is TanStack Devtools, not product UI; it also masks the fifth mobile tab.

## Questions to Consider

1. If branching is why Charon exists, why is its UI the smallest text on the screen — and what would "branch from here" be as a verb in the message menu?
2. Are Defaults, Providers and Presets three pages, or one idea in three vocabularies before the user can type a single token?
3. Where does the app ever tell a writer their words are safe? No save status in chat, no undo, no "stored on this server" beside the API key — yet the pitch is "one SQLite file you can copy".
